What's inside

Everything a personal expense log needs, nothing it doesn't.

No budgets you'll ignore, no bank credentials to hand over. Outlay covers the four things people actually want from an expense tracker — fast entry, smart sorting, honest reports, and full control of the data.

AI auto-categorization

Leave the category on "Auto" and Outlay reads the title and note, then files the expense under the right one of nine categories for you — no dropdown-hunting required.

Optional

Log an expense in seconds

Title, amount, date, and an optional note — that's the whole form. Add it from your phone between paying and walking away from the counter.

Visual spending reports

A dedicated reports page breaks your spending down by category and by month, so patterns become obvious instead of buried in a list.

Month-over-month comparison

Outlay automatically compares this month's total against last month's and shows the percentage swing, so you know immediately if spending crept up.

Private, password-protected accounts

Every account is its own walled garden. Passwords are salted and hashed before storage — never kept in plain text.

Edit or delete anytime

Mis-typed an amount or picked the wrong category? Every entry stays fully editable — nothing about your history is locked in.

The whole workflow

Three steps between "I just paid for that" and "it's logged."

Outlay is built around the moment right after you spend money — while the amount is still fresh in your head.

01

Create your account

Pick a username and password on the register page. Your expenses are scoped to your account only — nobody else's data mixes with yours.

02

Add an expense

From the dashboard, enter what it was for and how much. Leave the category on Auto and let the AI classifier pick from Food, Transport, Shopping, Bills, Entertainment, Health, Education, Business, or Other.

03

Check your reports

Open the reports page any time to see totals by category, spending by month, the change versus last month, and a short written insight on where the money's going.

Built to stay out of the way

Your data, your database, your rules.

Outlay doesn't connect to your bank and doesn't phone home to anyone else's server. It's a small Flask app with a local SQLite database — you run it, you own the file.

  • Hashed passwords

    Every password is salted and hashed with Werkzeug's security helpers before it ever touches the database.

  • One file, one database

    Everything lives in a single SQLite file. Back it up by copying it, move it by copying it — no external service required.

  • Expenses scoped per user

    Every query is filtered by the logged-in user's ID, so one account can never see another's entries.

# Every expense query is scoped like this
SELECT * FROM expenses
WHERE user_id = ? ORDER BY
  expense_date DESC

# Passwords are never stored as plain text
generate_password_hash(password)
Questions

Good to know before you start

Do I need to link a bank account?

No. Outlay is a manual expense log — you enter what you spent yourself. Nothing connects to a bank, card, or payment provider.

Is the AI categorization required?

No. Auto-categorization is a convenience, not a requirement. You can pick any of the nine categories yourself from the dropdown when you add an expense, and edit the category later at any time.

Where does my data live?

In a local SQLite database file alongside the app. There's no third-party analytics or cloud sync built in — the data goes only where you choose to host it.

Can multiple people use the same install?

Yes. Each person registers their own account, and every expense is tied to that account's user ID, so records never mix between users.

What happens if I mistype an amount?

Open the entry from your dashboard and edit the title, amount, category, note, or date — nothing is permanent until you delete it.
